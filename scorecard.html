What it measures

A coordinated financial life requires four things done well. Simultaneously.

The scorecard is organized around the same four-part framework Bannerman uses to coordinate connected financial decisions: Grow, Protect, Distribute, and Transfer.

Grow

↗

Investments, savings, equity compensation, tax-aware strategy, and the decisions that help wealth continue working.

Protect

◎

Risk, insurance, estate documents, family obligations, and the decisions that keep a plan from being quietly undermined.

Distribute

↓

Retirement income, withdrawal strategy, tax brackets, Social Security, Medicare surcharges, and turning assets into a usable plan.

Transfer

→

Legacy, estate coordination, beneficiaries, family conversations, and preparing wealth to move with less confusion.
