Our Modern Wealth framework

Modern wealth has more moving parts. They should be working together.

A strong financial life isn’t built from isolated decisions. Investments, taxes, retirement income, risk, estate planning, and family priorities all affect each other. Bannerman’s Modern Wealth framework organizes those decisions into four connected areas.

Grow

Investments, savings, equity compensation, tax-aware strategy, and the choices that help wealth continue working.

Protect

Risk, insurance, estate documents, family obligations, and the decisions that keep your plan from being quietly undermined.

Distribute

Retirement income, withdrawals, Social Security, tax brackets, Medicare surcharges, and turning assets into a usable plan.

Transfer

Legacy, estate coordination, beneficiaries, family conversations, and preparing wealth to move with less confusion.
