What it measures

The scorecard looks at four connected areas of financial freedom.

Behind the scenes, questions can still cover taxes, investing, protection, planning, and cash flow. On the page and in the report, we group the results into four clearer quadrants: Grow, Protect, Distribute, and Transfer.

Grow

↗

Investing, cash flow, savings behavior, equity compensation, and the choices that help wealth continue working.

Protect

◎

Insurance, estate documents, risk, concentration, and the areas that keep wealth from being quietly exposed.

Distribute

↓

Taxes, retirement income, withdrawals, and the strategy behind turning assets into usable financial freedom.

Transfer

→

Legacy, beneficiaries, estate coordination, family decisions, and preparing wealth to move with less confusion.

Your report

You’ll receive a clearer view of what deserves attention next.

The report should show the overall Financial Freedom Score, four quadrant scores, and a plain-English interpretation of where your financial picture may be working and where it may be leaking opportunity.
