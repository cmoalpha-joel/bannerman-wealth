The Bannerman Wealth Framework

A clearer way to coordinate the moving parts of wealth.

As your financial life grows, decisions start to overlap. Investments affect taxes. Retirement income affects cash flow. Estate decisions affect family conversations.

The Bannerman Wealth Framework helps you see how one decision affects another, then coordinate the whole picture through one planning relationship.

Four connected areas

The Bannerman Wealth Framework

The framework gives each planning conversation a clear structure while still showing how the decisions overlap. A concentrated stock position, retirement income decision, or family transition rarely lives in one category.

Grow

↗

How wealth continues working and accumulating over time.

  • ✓Investment strategy and portfolio design
  • ✓Cash flow, savings, and equity compensation
  • ✓Tax-aware accumulation decisions
Example: concentrated stock can create growth potential, but also tax timing and risk exposure.

Protect

◎

How wealth, income, family, and future plans are protected from avoidable risk.

  • ✓Insurance and risk review
  • ✓Estate documents and contingency planning
  • ✓Concentration, liability, and family exposure
Example: family decisions can affect beneficiaries, estate documents, insurance, and cash flow.

Distribute

↓

How assets become usable income and support real-life decisions.

  • ✓Retirement income and withdrawal strategy
  • ✓Tax brackets, Social Security, and Medicare considerations
  • ✓Coordinating spending, income, and portfolio design
Example: retirement income decisions affect taxes, health care costs, investments, and legacy goals.

Transfer

→

How wealth moves, what it supports, and how future decisions are prepared.

  • ✓Legacy and beneficiary coordination
  • ✓Family conversations and charitable intent
  • ✓Preparing wealth to move with less confusion
Example: transfer planning often connects estate documents, taxes, family dynamics, and long-term income needs.
How we work together

A planning relationship should have structure.

The process is designed to move from understanding your situation to building a coordinated plan, then helping keep that plan current as life changes.

Regular advice, implementation support, and planning updates as your financial life continues to evolve.
