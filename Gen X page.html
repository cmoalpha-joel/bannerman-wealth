What changes now

What you’ve built now needs a more coordinated plan.

Many Gen X households have already done the hard work of earning, saving, investing, and making thoughtful decisions. The next challenge is making sure retirement, taxes, risk, family, and legacy decisions are working from the same playbook.

More overlap

Investment, tax, retirement, estate, and family decisions are no longer separate conversations.

Less room for mistakes

Timing, taxes, risk, and withdrawal decisions can have longer-lasting consequences than they did earlier in life.

More people affected

Your choices may now affect your spouse, children, parents, beneficiaries, or future generations.

The Bannerman Wealth Framework

Four areas help organize the whole picture.

The framework helps organize the major planning decisions without treating investments, taxes, income, risk, and legacy as separate conversations.

Grow

↗

Investing, savings, cash flow, equity compensation, and tax-aware accumulation decisions.

Protect

◎

Insurance, estate documents, concentration risk, family obligations, and contingency planning.

Distribute

↓

Retirement income, withdrawals, tax brackets, Social Security, Medicare considerations, and spending strategy.

Transfer

→

Legacy decisions, beneficiaries, family conversations, charitable intent, and preparing wealth to move with less confusion.
